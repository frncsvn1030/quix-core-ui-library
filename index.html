What you get

Good-looking interfaces shouldn't be complicated

We kept things simple on purpose: clear class names, sensible defaults, and room to make it yours.

Ready when you are

Pick a component, copy the snippet, and drop it in. No setup ritual, no guesswork.

Light and considerate

Small, accessible pieces that respect your users and don't weigh your page down.

Yours to tweak

Variants, sizes and shapes are just classes, so it's easy to make it fit your own design.

A little backstory

Quix Core UI was an internship project developed at Qstrike by three UI/UX interns: Francis, Princess, and Jonathan, with Francis leading the development.

Our internship ended before we could finish all the components, so some pages are still unavailable. One year later, Francis has made a few updates to the homepage, but the overall theme and most of the website remain the same as they were during the internship.
